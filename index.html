Dear Kiru,
happy birthday, my favourite person

I don't think there is a version of my life in the last few years that doesn't have you loudly, chaotically, wonderfully in it. Somewhere between the coaching classes, the street food we definitely shouldn't have eaten twice in one day, and a thousand voice notes we've sent each other, you became the kind of friend people spend a lifetime hoping to find.

You are, without exaggeration, the most chaotic, kind, and beautiful soul I know — and I mean all three in the same breath, because that's exactly how you show up. Chaotic in the way you turn an ordinary evening into a story we'll be telling for years. Kind in the way you notice when someone's having a hard day before they've even said a word. Beautiful in every single way that actually matters.

Thank you for the gossip sessions that ran two hours too long, the laughs that left us both in actual pain, the inside jokes that make no sense to anyone else and every ounce of sense to us. Thank you for being someone I never have to explain myself to.

Here's to another year of your particular brand of magic — and to every memory we haven't made yet. I hope this year hands you everything you deserve, Kiru, and then a little more, just because.

I love you endlessly. Happiest of birthdays.

always, your person ✿
